How to use Tebow

First time here: click KEY in the top bar and paste your API key. Without it, nothing can answer.

Talk: tap the orb's center and speak. Pause when you're done — it sends itself and answers out loud. Tap again to turn the mic off.

Type: use the bar at the bottom and press Enter.

Six chats: the orb's colored slices are six separate conversations, each with its own memory. Click a slice, a tab, or a name in the Chats list to switch. Your question goes to the highlighted chat. A slice grows as its chat is used more.

Right panel: the conversation of the highlighted chat — what you said and what Tebow answered.

ASK ALL sends one question to all six chats at once. LIVE keeps the mic open hands-free. WAKE is hands-free but only obeys after "hey Tebow". VOICE mutes the speech. PURGE erases all six chats.

Mic asking permission? Click Allow. If it says MIC BLOCKED, click the padlock next to the web address, set Microphone to Allow, and reload the page.

Tablet mode: in KEY, check "remember on this device"; arm WAKE once. The screen stays awake while TEBOW is open, and after any restart one tap on the screen re-arms everything.

Click anywhere to close.
